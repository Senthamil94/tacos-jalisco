Our story

A family kitchen that never learned to cut corners.

Tacos Jalisco opened on Sonoma Blvd in 1986 and has been run by the same family ever since. The recipes travelled here from Jalisco, and they haven't been simplified for anyone: birria still braises for hours, salsas are made in-house each morning, and the tortillas come out warm because that's the only way they should arrive.

Come in for a quick lunch or bring twelve people for dinner. Either way you'll get the same generous plate, the same welcome, and the same food that has kept Vallejo coming back for four decades.

Four decades in, we still cook it the long way.

Since 1986, one family has run this kitchen — through three generations of Vallejo regulars, birthdays, quinceañeras and ordinary Tuesday lunches. The recipes haven't changed. Neither has the welcome.

01 Made to order, every order

Nothing sits under a heat lamp.

Meat is braised and grilled in small batches through the day. Salsas, guacamole and pico de gallo are cut fresh. Beans are cooked from dry. It takes longer, and that is exactly the point.

Portions are generous because we would rather send you home with a container than have you leave hungry. That's how it worked in 1986 and it's how it works now.
